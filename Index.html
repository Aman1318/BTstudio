Questions

Frequently asked questions

What services does BT Studio offer?

We provide professional video editing, graphic design, social media content creation, social media management, motion graphics, and AI-powered product videos.

How do I get started with BT Studio?

Simply contact us through our website. Share your requirements, project details, and goals. We’ll discuss the best creative solution for your brand.

What are your pricing options?

We offer both project-based pricing and monthly packages. Our pricing depends on the services, project requirements, and volume of work.

Who will be working on my projects?

Your projects are handled by our creative team, focusing on video editing, graphic design, and content creation to deliver professional results.

How long does a project usually take?

Project timelines depend on the type of service, complexity, and deliverables. We confirm the estimated delivery time before starting each project.

Do you offer ongoing creative support?

Yes. We offer monthly creative packages for brands and businesses that need consistent video editing, graphic design, and social media content.
